Fake-email protection, explained for your kind of business

A fake invoice, a "new bank details" note, a message that looks like it came from the owner. These emails work because they appear to come from a real company address. The scenario changes from one line of work to the next, though.

For a title company it is wire instructions. For a contractor it is a progress-payment invoice. For a nonprofit it is a donation or vendor payment. For a freight broker it is a changed carrier payment.

Pick your industry below. Each page shows what an impersonation looks like in your world, why small firms get picked, what closes the gap, and what it honestly does not cover. Each ends with a short checklist you can finish this week.

The fix is the same idea everywhere: a few settings at your domain host and your email provider. The pages explain it once, in plain English.
