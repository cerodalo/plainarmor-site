Privacy

Updated October 3, 2026

Who we are

Featherbrooke Inc. United States only. Contact us through support or support@plainarmor.com. We reply within one business day.

What we collect

  • Your email address and its domain, and what we find in the domain's public DNS records.
  • The code or domain you enter on our home page, and the time you entered it.
  • Your orders, and your messages to us.
  • Your IP address, in server logs and for rate limiting.

We never see your card number or passwords, and never log in to anything of yours. Mock emails are drawn on screen only and never sent.

Cookies

Three cookies, all HttpOnly, Secure and ours only: pa_email holds the email you entered for one hour; pa_v counts the domains you have tested for 24 hours (the free tests); pa_verified remembers for 30 days that you confirmed your email. No advertising or tracking cookies. Ticked steps stay in your browser only.

To limit abuse we also keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, never the address itself, with the domains tested from it, and after the free tests we email a one-time link to the address you entered to confirm it is yours; we keep these records for up to 30 days.

Sharing

Stripe (payments), and our hosting and email providers, get only what they need to serve you. We do not sell your data. We may disclose it if the law requires.

Keeping and deleting

We keep data while it is needed to serve you, and order records up to seven years for tax. Ask us through support to see, correct or delete your data. We reply within one business day and finish within 45 days.

Stop our emails

Unsubscribe or reply "stop".

Not for anyone under 18. Changes are dated above.
