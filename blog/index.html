Plain-English email security for small businesses

Most small businesses never think about whether a stranger can send an email that looks like it came from their own domain. Yet it is one of the simplest tricks used in invoice fraud and impersonation. These guides explain the problem without jargon, show you how to check your own domain, and walk through the fixes in the order that keeps your real email working.

You will find four kinds of articles: understanding the problem, step-by-step how-tos, short explainers of terms like SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and guides for deciding what to do next. Each post starts with a short summary, ends with key takeaways and common questions, and is written for people who are not technical. We are US-focused, we avoid hype, and we only cite numbers we could verify at the source.
