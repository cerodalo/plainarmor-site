About PlainArmor: Frequently Asked Questions

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

PlainArmor helps small businesses in the United States answer one question: could a stranger send an email that looks like it came from our company? We look at the public DNS records for your domain, the same information any mail server checks, and tell you whether your domain tells the world to reject fakes.

If there is a gap, we show you a mock fake email on screen so you can see the problem clearly. Nothing is sent. Then you can buy Fix it, a one-time set of step-by-step instructions that you follow yourself. We never touch your settings, and we make no promises we cannot keep. The answers below cover what PlainArmor is, who it is for, and what it does and does not do.

Questions and answers

What is PlainArmor?

PlainArmor is a US-only service that checks whether a stranger could send an email that looks like it came from your company's own domain. We read your public DNS records, show you the result, and sell step-by-step instructions you follow yourself to close the gap. It is built for small businesses without an IT person.

What problem does PlainArmor solve?

PlainArmor addresses email spoofing: someone sending a message that appears to come from your own domain. If your domain lacks a strong DMARC setting, mail systems have no instruction to reject those fakes. We find that gap and give you the steps to close it, in plain English.

Who is PlainArmor for?

PlainArmor is for US small businesses, typically one to fifty people, that have a company domain and email but no dedicated IT staff. If you wonder whether someone could send email pretending to be you, and you want clear steps rather than jargon, this is aimed at you.

Do I need technical skills to use PlainArmor?

No deep technical skills are needed, but you will need to log in to wherever your domain's DNS is managed and paste in a few values. The instructions are written for non-experts, one step at a time. If you get stuck, support replies within one business day.

Does PlainArmor work outside the United States?

No. PlainArmor is currently available to US businesses only. The service, the pricing and the support are built around US small businesses, so we do not offer it to companies elsewhere at this time. If that changes, we will say so on our site.

Does PlainArmor log in to my accounts or change my settings?

No. PlainArmor never logs in to your accounts and never touches your DNS or any other setting. We only read public records that anyone on the internet can look up. You make every change yourself, following the instructions, so you stay in control.

Does PlainArmor send fake emails to test my company?

No. PlainArmor never sends a fake email to you, your staff or anyone else. The fake message you see is a mock drawn on screen to show what a stranger could try. Nothing is delivered to any inbox, so there is nothing to clean up or explain to your team.

What do I get after the free check?

If your domain has a gap, you see an on-screen mock of a fake email that looks like it came from your company. From there you can buy Fix it, a one-time purchase of step-by-step instructions. If your setup already looks protected, there is nothing you need to buy to close a gap.

Is PlainArmor a replacement for an IT provider or security software?

No. PlainArmor does one narrow job: helping you stop strangers from sending email that looks like it came from your domain. It does not monitor your computers, scan for malware or protect mailboxes from being hacked. Those need other tools and good habits.

Does PlainArmor guarantee I will never be impersonated?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee that. A strong DMARC setting makes it much harder for strangers to send mail that looks like it is from your exact domain, but it does not stop lookalike domains or hacked mailboxes. We explain these limits openly.

How do I contact PlainArmor support?

You can contact support through the details on the PlainArmor site. Support replies within one business day. If you are partway through the instructions and something does not match what you see on screen, tell us what you see and we will help you through that step.
