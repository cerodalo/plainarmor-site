DMARC, SPF and DKIM Basics: Plain-English FAQ

What SPF, DKIM and DMARC are, how they work together, and what a small business needs to know about each, without the jargon.

What are SPF, DKIM and DMARC?
They are three DNS-based settings that help receivers judge whether email claiming to be from your domain is genuine.
What is SPF?
SPF, Sender Policy Framework, is a DNS record listing which servers may send email for your domain.
What is DKIM?
DKIM, DomainKeys Identified Mail, attaches a cryptographic signature to outgoing messages.
What is DMARC?
DMARC is a DNS record that ties SPF and DKIM to the From address people actually see and tells receivers what to do with failures: nothing, quarantine, or reject.
Why is DMARC needed if I already have SPF and DKIM?
SPF and DKIM can pass without matching the visible From address, and neither tells receivers to reject failures.
What are the DMARC policy options?
There are three: p=none asks receivers to take no action and just report, p=quarantine asks them to treat failing mail as suspicious, usually spam, and p=reject asks the…
Is p=none enough?
No, not for stopping spoofing.
What is alignment?
Alignment means the domain verified by SPF or DKIM matches the domain in the visible From address.
What are DMARC reports?
DMARC reports are summaries that receiving mail providers send to an address you name in your DMARC record.
Where do these records live?
They live in your domain's DNS as TXT records.
Can DMARC break my email?
Yes, if you enforce it before all your legitimate senders are authenticated.
Do I need all three?
For DMARC to pass, you need at least one of SPF or DKIM working and aligned; having both is better.
Do these settings protect against phishing in general?
Only partly.

Email Spoofing Basics: Plain-English FAQ

What email spoofing is, why a stranger can send mail that looks like yours, what it can lead to, and what does and does not stop it.

What is email spoofing?
Email spoofing is sending a message that falsely appears to come from someone else, such as your company's domain.
Can someone really send an email that looks like it came from my company?
Yes, if your domain has no strong DMARC setting.
Why is email so easy to fake?
The original email system trusted the sender to state who they were.
Why would someone fake my company's email?
Attackers use a trusted name to get a reply or a click.
Does spoofing mean my email account was hacked?
No.
Who is affected when my domain is spoofed?
Usually the people who receive the fake: your customers, vendors or staff.
What is the difference between spoofing and phishing?
Spoofing is the faking of a sender's identity.
What is a lookalike domain?
A lookalike domain is a different domain that resembles yours, such as one with a swapped or added letter.
Does DMARC stop all fake email?
No.
What is display-name spoofing?
Display-name spoofing is when the visible sender name says your company or owner's name, but the actual address is from an unrelated domain.
How can I tell whether my domain can be spoofed?
Check whether your domain has a DMARC record and whether its policy is quarantine or reject.
Should I warn my customers about spoofing?
A short, calm note is reasonable if you have reason to worry, such as a fake reaching a customer.

What PlainArmor looks at in your public DNS, what the mock fake email is, and what the result means for your small business domain.

What does PlainArmor check?
PlainArmor checks your domain's public DNS records for email authentication settings, mainly DMARC, and looks at related SPF and DKIM information.
How does the check work?
The check looks up your domain's public DNS records, the same lookups any mail server performs when a message arrives.
What is the mock fake email?
The mock fake email is an example message drawn on your screen showing what a stranger could try if your domain is not protected.
Is the fake email actually sent to my inbox or my staff?
No.
What result means my domain has a gap?
A gap usually means your domain has no DMARC record, or has one set to p=none, which only asks receivers to monitor and not to act.
What if my check says I am already protected?
If your DMARC setting already instructs receivers to reject or quarantine failing mail, the check will reflect that and you do not need to buy anything to close a gap.
Does the check look at my email content or contacts?
No.
Why does the check focus on DMARC?
DMARC is the setting that tells receiving mail systems what to do with messages that fail authentication for your domain.
How long does the check take?
The check itself is quick because it only reads public DNS records.
Can the check be wrong?
The check reads public records accurately, but it cannot see everything.
Does PlainArmor check lookalike domains?
No.
Do I need to own the domain to run a check?
Because the check reads only public records, anyone can look up any domain.
