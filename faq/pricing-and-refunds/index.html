PlainArmor Pricing and Refunds: FAQ

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

PlainArmor pricing is intentionally simple. Fix it, the step-by-step instructions that close the gap, is $199 one-time. There is one optional add-on: a quarterly re-test at $99 a year, billed yearly and renewing until you cancel.

All sales are final. You see whether your domain has a gap, and the on-screen mock fake email, before paying anything. You follow the steps yourself; we never touch your DNS. The answers below cover exactly what each option includes, what it does not, and our no-refund policy. If you are unsure which option you need, start with Fix it alone; the follow-ons can always be added later.

Questions and answers

How much does PlainArmor cost?

Fix it costs $199 one-time. One optional add-on exists: a quarterly re-test at $99 a year, billed yearly. The initial check that shows whether you have a gap is how you decide whether to buy.

What is Fix it?

Fix it is a $199 one-time purchase of step-by-step instructions that you follow yourself to close the gap that lets strangers send email that looks like it came from your domain. We never touch your DNS or settings. You get either a fast route or a careful staged route, depending on your setup.

Is Fix it a subscription?

No. Fix it is a one-time payment of $199. It does not renew and there is no monthly fee. The only recurring option is the separate, optional quarterly re-test at $99 a year, which you choose to add and can cancel.

What is the quarterly re-test and how is it billed?

The re-test is optional and costs $99 a year, billed yearly. We re-check your public DNS every quarter and tell you if a record changed. It renews each year until you cancel. It is separate from Fix it and not required.

Do I have to buy the re-test?

No. Fix it stands on its own. You can buy Fix it, follow the steps, and stop there. The re-test is for owners who want a later check that things are still in place.

Is there a refund?

No. All sales are final and PlainArmor does not offer refunds. That is why you see whether your domain has a gap, and the on-screen mock fake email, before you pay.

What if Fix it is not right for me?

PlainArmor does not offer refunds, so check the result for your domain before you buy. If you get stuck on a step, contact support: it replies within one business day and will help you through it.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The prices are the ones listed: $199 one-time for Fix it, and $99 a year for the optional quarterly re-test. Your own domain registrar or DNS host may have its own fees, which are separate from PlainArmor.

Does the price include someone doing the work for me?

No. PlainArmor sells instructions, not hands-on service. You follow the steps yourself, and we never log in to or change your DNS. Support can help if a step does not match what you see, but we do not make the changes for you.

Do I pay for the free check or the mock fake email?

No. Seeing whether your domain has a gap, and the on-screen mock fake email, comes before any purchase. You pay only if you decide to buy Fix it. Nothing is sent to anyone during the check, and we only read public DNS records.

Do prices apply outside the US?

PlainArmor is available to US businesses only, and the prices shown are in US dollars. We do not currently sell to businesses outside the United States. The service, the instructions and support are all designed around US small businesses, so we keep it focused.
