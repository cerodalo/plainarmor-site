What Is Email Authentication?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

Email authentication is the set of techniques that let a receiving system check whether a message really came from the domain it claims. The main three are SPF, which lists allowed sending servers, DKIM, which signs messages, and DMARC, which ties them to the visible From address and sets a policy.

For a small business, authentication matters because plain email has no built-in proof of sender. Publishing these records lets receivers distinguish your genuine mail from imitations of your exact domain. It does not cover lookalike domains or hacked accounts.

What to do: make sure SPF and DKIM work for every service that sends as you, then publish and gradually enforce DMARC.
