What Is DMARC Alignment?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

Alignment means the domain that SPF or DKIM verified matches the domain in the From address a person sees. DMARC passes only when SPF or DKIM passes and is aligned. Relaxed alignment allows a subdomain of the same organizational domain to match; strict requires an exact match.

For a small business, alignment is what stops a stranger from passing SPF or DKIM using their own domain while displaying yours. It is also the usual reason a third-party service fails DMARC: it signs or sends with its own domain instead of yours.

What to do: when you add a service that sends as you, set up its custom DKIM or sending domain so it signs with your domain, then send a test.
