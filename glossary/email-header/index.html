What Is an Email Header?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

An email header is the technical block of information attached to every message, usually hidden by default. It records the sender and recipient addresses, the servers the message passed through, and often the results of SPF, DKIM and DMARC checks.

For a small business, headers are how you find out why a message was treated the way it was, for example why a legitimate email went to spam or whether a suspicious one really came from you. Most mail apps offer a view original or show headers option.

What to do: when troubleshooting, look for the authentication results in the header of a received test message, and share the header with support if you need help interpreting it.
