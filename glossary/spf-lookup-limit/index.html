What Is the SPF Lookup Limit?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

The SPF lookup limit is a rule that an SPF check may trigger at most ten DNS lookups from terms such as include, a, mx and redirect. A record that needs more than that returns an error instead of a pass.

For a small business, the limit matters because each service you add to SPF with an include can consume several lookups. Adding many tools can push you over without any visible warning, and then legitimate mail may fail SPF, which can hurt DMARC results if DKIM is not also passing.

What to do: remove services you no longer use from SPF, rely on DKIM for as many senders as possible, and test your record after each change.
