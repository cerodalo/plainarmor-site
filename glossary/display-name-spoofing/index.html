What Is Display-Name Spoofing?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

Display-name spoofing is when the name shown as the sender, such as your owner's or manager's name, is real but the actual email address behind it belongs to an unrelated account, often a free webmail address. Many phone and inbox views show only the name.

For a small business, it matters because your domain is not involved at all, so DMARC on your domain cannot block it. These messages usually rely on urgency and a name staff recognize, for example a request to buy gift cards or change a payment.

What to do: teach staff to tap or hover to see the full address, mark outside senders clearly if your mail system allows it, and verify unusual requests by phone.
