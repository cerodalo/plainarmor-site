What Is Account Takeover?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

Account takeover means someone other than the owner has gained access to a real account, such as a business mailbox, usually by stealing or guessing a password. Messages sent from it are genuine mail from your real server.

For a small business, this matters because DMARC cannot help. A message sent from a hijacked mailbox passes SPF, DKIM and DMARC, because it truly comes from your systems. Takeover is a different problem from spoofing and needs different defenses.

What to do: turn on multi-factor sign-in for every mailbox, use unique strong passwords, watch for unexpected forwarding rules or sign-in alerts, and change the password and sign out all sessions if you suspect access.
