What Is MTA-STS?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

MTA-STS is a standard that lets your domain tell other mail servers to use encrypted connections when delivering email to you, and to refuse delivery if a secure connection cannot be made. It is published through a DNS record and a small policy file on a web address.

For a small business, MTA-STS is about protecting mail in transit, not about stopping fake senders. It is a different problem from the one DMARC solves, and it is optional extra hardening that most small domains can leave for later.

What to do: finish DMARC first. Consider MTA-STS afterward if your mail provider supports it and documents the setup, since a mistake can affect incoming mail.
