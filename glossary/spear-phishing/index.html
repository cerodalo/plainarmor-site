What Is Spear Phishing?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

Spear phishing is phishing aimed at a specific person or organization rather than sent to thousands at random. The attacker uses real details, such as a colleague's name, a vendor you use or a project you mentioned online, to make the message believable.

For a small business, spear phishing matters because owners, bookkeepers and office managers are easy to research from public sources, and a personalized request to pay an invoice or share a file can look routine. The sender may use a spoofed address, a lookalike domain or a name that only resembles someone you know.

What to do: set a simple rule that payment changes and sensitive requests are confirmed by a phone call to a known number, and keep DMARC enforced so fakes of your own exact domain are rejected.
