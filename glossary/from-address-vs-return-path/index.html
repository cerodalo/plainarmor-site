From Address vs Return-Path: What Is the Difference?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

The From address is the sender shown to the reader. The Return-Path, also called the envelope sender or MAIL FROM, is a separate hidden address where bounces are sent, and it is what SPF checks. The two can be different domains.

For a small business, this gap is why SPF alone does not stop spoofing: a stranger can pass SPF with their own Return-Path domain while showing your domain in the From line. DMARC closes the gap by requiring that the checked domain aligns with the visible From domain.

What to do: when a service sends as you, make sure it is configured so its DKIM signature or Return-Path uses your domain, not only its own.
