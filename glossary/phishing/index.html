What Is Phishing?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

Phishing is a message, usually an email, designed to trick the reader into handing over money, passwords or private information. It often pretends to be a trusted company, a boss or a vendor, and asks the reader to click a link, open a file or act quickly.

For a small business, phishing matters because one convincing message to one person can be enough, and small teams rarely have a security staff to catch it. Phishing messages can use a spoofed sender, a lookalike domain or a hacked account, so no single setting stops them all.

What to do: combine email authentication such as DMARC with staff habits: verify unusual requests by another channel, use multi-factor sign-in, and report suspicious messages rather than deleting them silently.
