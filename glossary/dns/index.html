What Is DNS?

By The PlainArmor Team · Last reviewed October 3, 2026

DNS, the Domain Name System, is the internet's address book. It stores records that connect your domain name to the services behind it, such as your website, your mail servers and your email security settings.

For a small business, DNS matters because your email protection is published there. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are all DNS records, and they are public, which is why anyone, including PlainArmor, can read them. It also means a mistaken edit can disrupt your website or mail, so changes deserve care.

What to do: find out who hosts your DNS, keep a copy of your current records before any change, and know who has login access to make edits.
