Fix it: $199, one time

Fix it is a one-time purchase of step-by-step instructions that you follow yourself to close the gap that lets strangers send email that looks like it came from your domain. It does not renew and there is no monthly fee.

Fix it includes:

  • Instructions written in plain English, one step at a time, with the exact values to enter.
  • A route matched to your setup: the fast route for businesses where no other services send email as the domain (about 20 days to the strictest setting), or the careful staged route where other services send as you (checkpoints at days 7, 14, 21 and 28, always checking that mail still works first).
  • A one-step rollback, so you can undo a change if something breaks.
  • Support that replies within one business day.

What is not included

Being clear about limits is part of the deal.

  • We do not make the changes for you. We never log in to or change your DNS. You follow the steps, or pass them to whoever manages your domain.
  • We do not protect against everything. A strong DMARC setting does not stop lookalike domains, display-name tricks or hacked mailboxes.
  • We do not guarantee outcomes. No one can honestly promise you will never be impersonated.
  • We do not cover other fees. Your domain registrar, DNS host or mail provider may have their own charges, separate from PlainArmor.
  • We do not offer legal or compliance advice.

No refunds

All sales are final: PlainArmor does not offer refunds. That is why the result for your domain, and the on-screen mock fake email, come before you pay. If you get stuck on a step, support replies within one business day.

Which option fits

If you want the problem fixed and you are comfortable following instructions, Fix it is the answer. If you would rather hand it on, pass the steps to whoever manages your domain.
